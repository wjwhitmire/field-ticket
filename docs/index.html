Method and limits

Money. Every amount is stored and added as a whole number of cents. Quantities carry up to 3 decimals, discounts up to 2, and the tax rate up to 3. They are turned into whole numbers before any math, so no rounding error builds up.

Rounding. Each division that lands on cents rounds to the nearest cent, and an exact half cent rounds away from zero (0.5 cent becomes 1 cent).

Line. amount = round(qty x rate), then line discount = round(amount x disc% / 100) and net = amount - line discount.

Document. subtotal = sum of line nets. A percent discount is round(subtotal x pct / 100); a dollar discount is capped at the subtotal. The document discount is spread over the lines in proportion to their nets (largest remainder, ties to the earlier line), so each taxable line is taxed on its share after discount.

Tax. Per document: tax = round(sum of taxable bases x rate / 100), rounded once. Per line: tax = sum of round(base x rate / 100), rounded on every line. The two can differ by a few cents; pick the one your state or customer expects.

Total and balance. total = subtotal - discount + tax + shipping. balance = total - deposit - payments. A negative balance is a credit.

Due date. Issue date plus 0 (Due on receipt), 15, 30, or 60 calendar days, on the Gregorian calendar with leap years. An invoice marked Sent is overdue from the day after its due date. It is Paid when marked Paid or when deposit and payments cover the total.

Hours. Each day row is end minus start on a 24 hour clock. An end at or before the start means the shift ran past midnight, so 24 hours are added; equal times count as a full 24 hour day. No overtime split. Hours show with 2 decimals; a labor line made from them uses 3 decimals (20 minutes is 0.333 h).

Limits.

  • Not tax or legal advice. One tax rate per document. No compound or multi-jurisdiction tax, no tax on shipping, no withholding.
  • US dollars only. Any one amount and any one line is capped at $10,000,000.00.
  • Data is stored in this browser's local storage. Clearing site data, private windows, or some phone storage cleanups erase it. Export a backup often.
  • The PDF uses the built-in Helvetica font, which covers Western European letters only. Other characters print as a question mark.
  • The PDF is built on your device by jsPDF, an MIT licensed library loaded once from cdnjs with an integrity hash. No document data is sent anywhere.
  • A drawn signature is a picture of a signature, not a verified electronic signature.
